SECTION 1: ACCOUNT OPENING AND MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

1.1 ELIGIBILITY
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Valid government-issued ID required
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or government letter)
- Social Security Number (SSN) or Tax Identification Number (TIN)
- Must not be on any sanctions list

1.2 ACCOUNT TYPES
Standard Account:
- Default account for all new customers
- No minimum balance requirement
- No monthly maintenance fee
- Earns 1.5% annual interest on balances over $1,000
- Free domestic transfers
- $5 international wire transfer fee

Premium Account:
- Requires minimum balance of $10,000
- $25 monthly maintenance fee (waived if balance > $25,000)
- Earns 4.5% annual interest
- Unlimited free domestic transfers
- Free international wire transfers
- Dedicated customer support line
- Priority transaction processing

Student Account:
- Available for customers aged 18-24
- No minimum balance
- No monthly fee
- Earns 2.0% annual interest
- Limited to 5 free withdrawals per month
- $2 fee for additional withdrawals

1.3 DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR KYC (KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER)
Primary Identification (one required):
- Passport (valid, not expired)
- State-issued driver's license
- National ID card
- Military ID

Proof of Address (one required, issued within last 60 days):
- Utility bill (electricity, water, gas, internet)
- Bank statement showing name and address
- Mortgage statement
- Lease agreement
- Property tax bill

Additional Verification (if required):
- Social Security card or Tax ID documentation
- Employment verification letter
- Source of funds declaration for large deposits (>$10,000)

1.4 ACCOUNT APPROVAL PROCESS
- All new accounts require manual admin approval
- Standard processing time: 1-2 business days
- You will receive email notification at each stage:
  * "Application Received" - immediately after signup
  * "Under Review" - when admin begins review
  * "Approved" or "Rejected" - final decision
- Pending accounts have limited access (see Section 1.5)
- If rejected, you may reapply with corrected information
- Rejection reasons: incomplete documents, verification failure, sanctions list match

1.5 ACCOUNT STATUS AND ACCESS LEVELS
PENDING STATUS:
- Can view balance and account details
- Can view transaction history
- CANNOT make transfers or deposits
- CANNOT access admin features
- Cannot update profile until approved

APPROVED STATUS:
- Full access to all features
- Can make transfers and deposits
- Can update profile information
- Can access all pages and features

REJECTED STATUS:
- Cannot access banking features
- Must reapply with corrected information
- Cannot create new account with same email for 30 days

1.6 PROFILE COMPLETION
- Required after first login (especially for Google OAuth users)
- Required information: full address
- Optional: phone number, date of birth
- Profile must be complete before transfers/deposits are enabled
- Admin reviews address for accuracy
- Updates to address require re-approval

SECTION 2: TRANSACTIONS - TRANSFERS
================================================================================

2.1 HOW TO MAKE A TRANSFER
1. Navigate to the Transfers page
2. Enter the recipient's account number
3. Enter the transfer amount
4. Add an optional description
5. Review and confirm
6. Enter your password or 2FA code if prompted
7. Wait for confirmation

2.2 TRANSFER RULES
- Cannot transfer to your own account
- Account must be ACTIVE status
- Sufficient balance required
- Transfers are final and cannot be cancelled once completed
- Minimum transfer amount: $1.00
- Maximum single transfer amount: $5,000 (Standard), $15,000 (Premium)

2.3 TRANSFER LIMITS
Standard Account Holder:
- Daily limit: $10,000
- Monthly limit: $50,000
- Single transaction limit: $5,000
- Maximum 10 transfers per day

Premium Account Holder:
- Daily limit: $25,000
- Monthly limit: $250,000
- Single transaction limit: $15,000
- Unlimited transfers per day

Admin Account:
- Daily limit: $50,000
- Monthly limit: $500,000
- Single transaction limit: $25,000
- Unlimited transfers per day

2.4 TRANSFER FEES
Domestic Transfers:
- Standard to Standard: Free
- Premium to Premium: Free
- Standard to Premium: Free
- All domestic transfers: No fee

International Transfers:
- Standard speed (3-5 business days): $15 fee
- Express speed (1-2 business days): $35 fee
- Currency conversion fee: 1% of amount
- Minimum international transfer: $100

2.5 FAILED TRANSFER REASONS
Common reasons for transfer failure:
1. Insufficient balance
2. Daily or monthly limit exceeded
3. Account not ACTIVE
4. Recipient account not found
5. Transfer to self
6. Amount below minimum ($1.00)
7. Amount above maximum or limit
8. System maintenance window

2.6 TRANSFER PROCESSING TIMES
- Domestic transfers: Instant to 1 business day
- International transfers: 1-5 business days depending on speed
- Transfers initiated after 5 PM EST: Processed next business day
- Weekend transfers: Processed next business day

SECTION 3: TRANSACTIONS - DEPOSITS
================================================================================

3.1 HOW TO MAKE A DEPOSIT
1. Navigate to the Deposit page
2. Select deposit method (Cash, Check, Wire)
3. Enter amount
4. Add optional description
5. Confirm

3.2 DEPOSIT METHODS
Cash Deposit:
- Available immediately
- No limit on amount
- Can be made at any partner ATM or branch

Check Deposit:
- Funds availability: 2-3 business days
- First $5,000 available next business day
- Amount over $5,000 held for 7 business days
- Maximum check deposit: $10,000 per check
- Must be endorsed by account holder

Wire Transfer Deposit:
- Funds availability: Same business day (if received before 3 PM EST)
- No limit on amount
- Sender pays outgoing wire fee
- Incoming wires: $10 fee for amounts under $10,000, free for $10,000+
- Requires sender to have your account number and bank routing number

3.3 DEPOSIT RULES
- Account must be ACTIVE
- Pending accounts CANNOT make deposits
- Deposits must be in USD
- Third-party checks not accepted
- Post-dated checks not accepted
- Deposits over $10,000 require additional verification

SECTION 4: ACCOUNT BALANCE AND TRANSACTION HISTORY
================================================================================

4.1 VIEWING BALANCE
- Dashboard shows current balance in real-time
- Balance updates immediately after transactions
- Click balance for detailed breakdown:
  * Available balance
  * Pending deposits
  * Pending withdrawals
  * Held funds

4.2 VIEWING TRANSACTIONS
- Navigate to Transactions page
- See all transactions in reverse chronological order
- Filter by date range (last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, custom)
- Search by keyword in description
- Export to CSV (last 500 transactions)

4.3 TRANSACTION RECORDS
Each transaction record includes:
- Transaction ID (unique identifier)
- Date and time
- Description (user-provided or system-generated)
- Amount
- Type: CREDIT (money in) or DEBIT (money out)
- Balance after transaction
- Operation ID (groups related transfers)

4.4 TRANSACTION STATUSES
COMPLETED:
- Transaction fully processed
- Balance updated
- Cannot be reversed

PENDING:
- Transaction initiated but not yet settled
- Balance may not yet reflect transaction
- Typically resolves within 1 business day

FAILED:
- Transaction could not be completed
- Common reasons: insufficient funds, limit exceeded, account inactive
- Balance not affected

SECTION 5: SECURITY AND FRAUD PREVENTION
================================================================================

5.1 ACCOUNT SECURITY BEST PRACTICES
- Never share your password with anyone
- Never share your OTP (One-Time Password)
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) when available
- Log out after using shared or public devices
- Monitor your account regularly
- Review transaction history weekly
- Report suspicious activity immediately

5.2 SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY MONITORING
- System monitors for unusual patterns 24/7
- Alerts triggered for:
  * Transactions over $1,000
  * Multiple failed login attempts
  * Login from new device or location
  * Rapid sequence of transactions
- You will be notified via SMS and email
- Temporary card freeze may be applied automatically
- You can review and approve/decline flagged transactions

5.3 WHAT TO DO IF YOU SUSPECT FRAUD
1. Immediately note the suspicious transaction details
2. Contact customer support: 1-800-REDMATH (option 2 for fraud)
3. Request immediate account freeze
4. Review all recent transactions
5. File a dispute for unauthorized transactions
6. Change your password immediately
7. Enable 2FA if not already enabled

5.4 PHISHING AWARENESS
RedMath Bank will NEVER:
- Ask for your password or OTP via email or phone
- Send links to login pages (always use our official app or website)
- Ask for your full credit card number via email
- Demand immediate action under threat of account closure

If you receive a suspicious message:
1. Do not click any links
2. Do not reply with personal information
3. Forward the message to phishing@redmath.com
4. Delete the message
5. Call us directly to verify: 1-800-REDMATH

5.5 PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum 8 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter
- Must include at least one lowercase letter
- Must include at least one number
- Must include at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot reuse last 5 passwords
- Cannot contain your email or name
- Expires every 90 days
- Account locks after 5 failed login attempts (unlocks after 30 minutes)
